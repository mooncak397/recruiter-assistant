This site is an AI assistant that answers questions about Christopher's CV. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser's local storage or session storage. There is no tracking, no analytics, no advertising and no user account. Please do not type personal details into the chat.

4. Security check (Cloudflare Turnstile)

Before the chat starts and before you send the contact form, Cloudflare Turnstile (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) checks that a human is using the page. For this, your browser loads a script from Cloudflare when the page opens. Cloudflare processes your IP address and technical signals from your browser and device. Our server sends the check result together with your IP address to Cloudflare for confirmation. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our interest being protection against bots and abuse. Access to information on your device is strictly necessary for this (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Details: Cloudflare privacy policy.

5. Chat

Your questions go to our server at Render (Render Services, Inc., US region). The server keeps the history of your chat session in memory only, for at most 30 minutes. After that it is deleted, and it is also deleted whenever the server restarts. Your browser holds only a random session token in the page's memory. It is gone when you reload or close the page.

For every answer, the server sends your question, the earlier part of the session (at most the last ten questions and answers) and Christopher's CV to Google's Gemini API (Google LLC, USA). Under the Gemini API Additional Terms (effective 2026-03-23), the data rules of the paid services apply to operators in the European Economic Area: Google does not use prompts and responses to improve its products. Google does log them for a limited time to detect violations of its usage policies, for safety and security, and for legally required disclosures. This data may be stored briefly in any country where Google or its agents have facilities. Details: Google privacy policy.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our interest being to answer questions about Christopher's professional background. If you ask in the context of a possible job or collaboration, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to a contract) applies as well. The AI only answers questions. It makes no decisions about you (no automated decision-making under Art. 22 GDPR).

6. Contact form

When you send the contact form, our server emails your email address and your message to Christopher through the email service Resend (USA). The AI does not see these details, and our server does not store them. The chat session only records that a form was sent. Resend keeps delivery logs for a limited time. Christopher keeps your email for as long as your request needs it. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR when the message concerns a possible collaboration, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our interest in answering requests). Details: Resend privacy policy.

7. Server logs and abuse protection

Our application writes no chat text, no email addresses and no IP addresses to its logs. A log entry for a chat answer holds only technical values such as the answer type, the language and the duration. The web server also logs each request with the time, path, status code and network address. On the free plan, Render keeps these logs for 7 days.

To limit abuse, the server counts requests per IP address (windows of one hour or one day). These counters exist in memory only and are lost at the latest when the server next restarts. On the free plan the server goes to sleep after 15 minutes without requests and restarts on the next visit. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our interest being safe and stable operation.
